How it works

From profile to application-ready
in three steps

1
Build your profile
Enter your location, sector, SC/ST status, income, and business details. SchemeRoute uses GPS or manual entry — no paperwork needed to get started.
2
Get matched schemes
The rule engine compares your profile against every eligible scheme — showing you a match percentage, the reasons behind it, and alternatives if you don't qualify.
3
Apply with a plan
Get your EMI calculations, a personalized document checklist, and AI-powered guidance — all linked to the official application portal.

Platform features

Everything in one place

Six capabilities that cover every gap between an entrepreneur and their first loan.

📍
Location Discovery

Auto-detects your state and district via GPS, then pulls all eligible SCA and Central schemes for your area. Manual entry if GPS is off.

🎯
Adaptive Scheme Finder

Scores your profile against each scheme's actual eligibility criteria — age, SC status, income, sector, project cost — and tells you exactly why you qualify or don't.

🧮
Financial Calculator

Calculates loan amounts, your contribution share, interest, monthly EMI, and total repayment — per scheme, not as a generic estimate.

📋
Document Handler

Generates a personalized document checklist for your selected scheme. Detects what's missing and links to every official source.

🤖
AI Advisor

Llama 3.2 3B answers your questions and explains eligibility in plain language — but never makes eligibility calls or crunches numbers itself.

🗄️
Live Scheme Database

JSON-based scheme store with state-level localization. New schemes and updates go live without touching the codebase — built to stay current.

9-Step Data Pipeline
  1. 1
    Profile collection — SC status, income, sector, project cost
  2. 2
    Location resolution — GPS → Nominatim → state + district
  3. 3
    Scheme retrieval — MongoDB query by location + sector + category
  4. 4
    Rule engine evaluation — eligibility scored against JSON criteria
  5. 5
    Match score + reasons — percentage, eligibility or rejection rationale
  6. 6
    Financial engine — EMI, contribution, total repayment per scheme
  7. 7
    Document checklist — scheme-specific, with missed-doc detection
  8. 8
    AI advisory layer — Llama 3.2 3B explains and guides
  9. 9
    Application guidance — links to official portals and next steps
